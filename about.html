About

Quotations from the Reformed tradition, cited and arranged by topic.

5 Solas Quotes gathers quotations from Reformed, Puritan and Particular Baptist writers, along with the confessions and other voices of the wider Reformed world. The aim is simple: to put the best of these old writers within easy reach, organized by doctrine, so that a reader can find what the church has said on a question and see it in the author's own words.

The name comes from the five solas of the Reformation: Scripture alone, grace alone, faith alone, Christ alone, to the glory of God alone.

How the site works

Every quotation is cited to its author, work and location. Where a free public copy of the work exists online, a link is given so you can read the quotation in its context. Quotations are drawn from public-domain works and published translations.

Browse by topic, by author, or through the longer collections, or use search. Each quotation has a Copy button, a Share on X button that posts the whole quotation with its citation, and a permanent link of its own. Please keep the citation with the quotation when you share it.

Quotations are included for study, including views the editor does not hold, such as those on baptism and the covenants. A quotation appearing here is a record of what the writer said, not an endorsement of every point.
