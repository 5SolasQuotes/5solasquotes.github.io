About

Quotations, mainly from the Reformed tradition, cited and arranged by topic.

Quotations mainly from Reformed, Puritan and Particular Baptist writers, along with church fathers and others, even outside the Reformed tradition, when they state a truth worth quoting. Arranged by doctrine and cited to their sources. The name comes from the five solas of the Reformation: Scripture alone, grace alone, faith alone, Christ alone, to the glory of God alone.
