Balcanquhall, Walter

1 quotation · Works cited: The Honor of Christian Churches & the Necessity of Frequenting of Divine Service & Public Prayers in them. Delivered in a Sermon at White-Hall before the King’s most excellent Majesty… (London, 1633), p. 14 Balcanquhall (1586?–1645) was a Scottish clergyman who became a staunch royalist and supporter of the church policy of Charles I of England (which was fundamentally Anglican), Balcanquhall being the author the defense of the King’s actions against the Scots in the 2nd Reformation, His Majestie’s Large Declaration concerning the Late Tumults in Scotland (1639).
