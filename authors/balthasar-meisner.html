For they are neither to be expelled indiscriminately, nor are they to be tolerated without limit; both of which must be declared further in a few words: | Many special laws could be recounted; we shall note only the principal ones: | 6. Jews are to be held as the servants of servants, and therefore they are to be treated harshly and compelled to perform services, to chop wood, to carry away dung and to perform other rustic labors. For they were sent by God into exile and delivered into servitude on account of the crucifixion of his Son and the contempt of the Gospel. Therefore, so that they may know that they are slaves, and may not boast on account of the prerogative of their seed, nor be emboldened in their blasphemies, the harshest burdens must be imposed upon them, nor should it be permitted that they remain idle and live exempt from labors.
