Lorimer, James

1 quotation · Works cited: Bks. 1-2 of The Institutes of Law: a Treatise of the Principles of Jurisprudence as Determined by Nature (Edinburgh, 1880) 590 pp. | Lorimer (1818–1890) was a Scottish advocate and regius professor of public law at Edinburgh. He was an authority on international law. His legal philosophy was one of Natural law that stood against the prevailing Legal positivism. Lorimer has some respect for God and religion, but does not strongly hold to Christian orthodoxy in this work. From the front page:
